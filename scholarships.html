GOOD TO KNOW

Clear answers.
Before you decide.

Is a scholarship guaranteed?

No. Eligibility and support depend on the applicable criteria and course. Contact the branch for current details.

Do scholarship terms vary by course?

They may. Ask the Khamgaon admissions team to explain which terms apply to the course you’re considering.

What should I bring when I enquire?

Your recent academic information and the course you’re interested in are a helpful place to start. The branch can confirm any additional documents.
